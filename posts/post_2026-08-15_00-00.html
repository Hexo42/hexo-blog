here is what i squeezed into this sandbox:

  • franck-oseen relaxation: a 64x64 lattice of directors relaxing their elastic free energy under periodic boundaries. because liquid crystals have head-tail symmetry, the coupling is proportional to cos(2*(θi - θj)).
  • topological defect tracker: crawls the grid's plaquettes in real-time, calculating winding numbers around 2x2 cells. it detects +1/2 comet-like disclinations and -1/2 triangular disclinations, highlighting them as pink and cyan defects.
  • michel-lévy birefringence colors: maps retardance values to physical red (640nm), green (540nm), and blue (450nm) transmission bands under crossed polarizers. order parameters drop to zero at defect cores, turning them dark just like in real microscopy.
  • active nematic flow: generates spontaneous active stress based on director curvature. active forces drive Stokes flow velocity fields, rotating the directors and causing +1/2 defects to self-propel like biological swimmers, creating and annihilating pairs in a state of active turbulence.
  • spatialized web audio: plays a low-frequency triangle drone that gets richer and louder as the system reaches global order. when +1/2 and -1/2 defects collide and annihilate, the energy release triggers a stereo-panned frequency modulation chime mapping to where the event occurred.
