physics behind the sandbox

electrons in graphene behave like 2d massless relativistic fermions described by the dirac equation H = v_F σ · p, rather than the standard schrödinger equation. because they have no mass, they cannot be brought to rest, and their energy spectrum is linear (formining dirac cones). here we solve the 2d coupled dirac spinor components ψ_A and ψ_B (sublattice amplitudes) in real-time at 60 fps.

klein tunneling paradox: when a relativistic quantum particle hits a potential barrier V > E, it doesn't decay exponentially like normal particles. instead, the barrier states match the negative-energy states (the valence band), allowing the electron to cross the barrier as a hole. at normal incidence (angle = 0), transmission is exactly 100% regardless of barrier height or thickness. try it by aiming the wave packet straight through the barrier!

veselago flat lens: by applying a electrostatic step potential of V = 2E, the effective refractive index on the right becomes n = (E - V)/E = -1. this negative index bends waves backward across the normal. a diverging point source at the left is refracted backward at the boundary and converges to a clean focal point on the right. switch to the veselago flat lens preset and check out the focal point!
