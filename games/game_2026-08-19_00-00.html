PHYSICS & INTERACTION GUIDE

This simulation solves the Landau-Lifshitz-Gilbert (LLG) equation augmented with Spin-Transfer Torque (STT) terms. It models a two-dimensional magnetic thin film where local atomic spins interact via quantum exchange, spin-orbit induced Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interactions (DMI), magnetic anisotropy, and an external magnetic field.

Key Phenomena to Explore:

  • Skyrmion Stability: Toggle between the Single Skyrmion and Reset to Ferromagnet. If you decrease DMI (D) below ~0.25, the skyrmions collapse into the background ferromagnetic state. If you increase DMI above ~0.45 or lower the magnetic field (B_z), skyrmions will stretch and expand into a labyrinth of helical magnetic stripes.
  • Skyrmion Hall Effect: Apply horizontal spin current using the Current u_x slider or the Left/Right Arrow keys. Due to their topological charge (Q ≈ -1), the skyrmions do not move straight; they get deflected diagonally. This transverse deflection is the Magnus-like topological Hall effect of skyrmions.
  • Racetrack Memory Guide: Click the Racetrack Memory preset. The grey horizontal stripes are non-magnetic defects (impurities painted on the track) which create a physical channel. Apply current pulses using the arrow keys to watch skyrmions slide down the track. When they hit the channel boundaries, the boundary repulsion counteracts the Skyrmion Hall deflection, guiding them straight!
  • Topological Charge (Winding Number): Select the Topological Charge view mode. You will see red/blue rings representing localized chiral winding. The total winding number (displayed in the stats) stays close to integers (e.g. -1.0 per skyrmion), reflecting its topological protection against continuous deformations.
  • Sonification: Enable audio. Move your cursor over the canvas. The pitch and timbre of the synthesizer are dynamically modulated by the local spin vector $(S_x, S_y, S_z)$ and topological charge density directly under the cursor, letting you "hear" the magnetic structure.
