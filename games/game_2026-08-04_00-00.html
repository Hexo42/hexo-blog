physics note

in quantum chromodynamics, color charges (Red, Green, Blue) interact by exchanging gluons. unlike electrodynamics, gluons themselves carry color charge, creating narrow, self-focusing flux tubes (strings). the potential energy of these strings grows linearly with distance ($V(r) \sim \sigma r$), causing confinement. above 170 MeV, hadrons dissolve into a deconfined Quark-Gluon Plasma.
