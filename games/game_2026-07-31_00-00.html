Chamber Signatures

Proton (p⁺) / Antiproton (p⁻)
Mass: 938.27 MeV/c² | Charge: +1 / -1
heavy baryonic particle. leaves thick, straight, highly ionized tracks due to massive stopping power. bends slowly in magnetic fields.
stopping power: -dE/dx ∝ z² / v²
Electron (e⁻) / Positron (e⁺)
Mass: 0.511 MeV/c² | Charge: -1 / +1
extremely light lepton. curves into tight, beautiful spirals. in heavy materials, they lose energy violently by emitting synchrotron / bremsstrahlung photons.
radiation loss: P ∝ q²a²γ⁴
Muon (μ⁻) / Antimuon (μ⁺)
Mass: 105.66 MeV/c² | Charge: -1 / +1
second-generation lepton. behaves like a heavy electron but doesn't bremsstrahlung easily. travels far, then decays into an electron/positron (visible bend/kink) and invisible neutrinos.
decay: μ⁻ → e⁻ + ν_bar_e + ν_μ
Charged Pion (π⁺ / π⁻)
Mass: 139.57 MeV/c² | Charge: +1 / -1
light meson. produced abundantly in nuclear collisions. leaves intermediate tracks, decaying rapidly into muons and neutrinos.
decay: π⁺ → μ⁺ + ν_μ
Gamma Photon (γ)
Mass: 0 | Charge: 0
neutral gauge boson. leaves no bubble tracks. however, in the coulomb field of heavy nuclei (like a lead absorber), it can spontaneously undergo pair production, spawning an e⁻/e⁺ double spiral.
threshold: E_γ > 2 m_e c² ≈ 1.02 MeV
Neutral Kaon (K⁰)
Mass: 497.61 MeV/c² | Charge: 0
strange neutral meson. travels invisibly, then decays into two charged pions (π⁺ and π⁻). this creates a signature "V-track" popping out of absolute vacuum.
decay: K⁰ → π⁺ + π⁻
