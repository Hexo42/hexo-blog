quantum levitation theory

Type-II superconductors cooled below their critical temperature ($T_c$) expel magnetic fields via the Meissner Effect (Zero-Field Cooled).

If cooled in an active magnetic field (Field Cooled), magnetic flux lines penetrate in discrete quantum threads called Abrikosov Vortices. The superconductor's defects lock these vortices, producing Flux Pinning. This resists any movement away from the frozen field lines, allowing it to lock in 3D space.
