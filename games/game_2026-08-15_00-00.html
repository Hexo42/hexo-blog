how to play

liquid crystals have head-tail symmetry. their orientation angle θ aligns with neighbors, but can support disclinations (topological defects) with half-integer charges.

  • drag on canvas: manually orient molecules.
  • click: tap to perturb and seed defects.
  • schlieren polarizer view: rotates dark brushes as you turn the polarizers. colors mimic genuine michel-lévy birefringence charts.
  • active nematic mode: drives flow based on director curvature, creating spontaneous defect pairs that swim and annihilate.
